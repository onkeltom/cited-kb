## Summary
Avery Quinn, an editor at Northwind Notes, describes a synthetic newsroom that bought a writing tool, counted how often people opened it, and treated that count as proof the work had changed. The episode is invented so this knowledge base can be searched before any real source is fetched.

## Claims
- [00:40] The newsroom measured how often people opened the tool and then called that count adoption (Avery Quinn)
- [01:20] Credit for the change went to the tool, while the real change was a checklist the editors rewrote by hand (Avery Quinn)
- [02:00] Nobody went back to see whether the task they said they cared about was actually finished (Avery Quinn)

## Evidence
- [02:40] The only figure stated is a login count; no count of finished tasks appears (Avery Quinn)
